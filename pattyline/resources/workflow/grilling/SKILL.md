---
name: grilling
description: Clarify a new idea or incomplete adopted work before writing an executable specification.
---

Read the supplied decision frontier and source documents. Ask the human about
execution-relevant product choices, one related group at a time. Research
environmental facts through permitted read-only tools instead of asking the
human to guess. Invoke the supplied domain-modeling instructions when vocabulary
or consequential trade-offs emerge. Preserve settled decisions and unresolved
questions explicitly in the structured response.

Cover actors, observable outcomes, failure behavior, scope, dependencies,
acceptance evidence, platforms, permissions, verification and routing/spending.
Use ../templates.md (provided as templates) for the response shape.
Finish the interview only when every execution-relevant choice is settled and
the human confirms the shared understanding. Return product questions to the
human; ordinary drafting repairs belong to the agent.
