---
name: preparation-review
description: Independently assess a proposed specification and breakdown when the human opts in.
---

Use a fresh read-only cloud session. Compare confirmed decisions, specification,
ticket outcomes, dependencies and executable verification. Report missing
requirements, contradictions, oversized slices, invented edges and unsupported
evidence claims. Return approved, changes-required with concrete drafting fixes,
or blocked with product questions. Agents repair drafting issues; the human
resolves product choices and approves the final breakdown and exact tickets.
This optional stage has no effect on mandatory independent implementation review.
