name: domain-modeling
description: Record settled domain language and consequential preparation decisions.
---

Extract terms from confirmed decisions. Define each in one or two concrete
sentences and identify ambiguous alternatives. Read the glossary and ADR
templates supplied with this session. Return proposed glossary entries and
concise ADR drafts with context, decision and consequences, preserving conflicts
as open questions. Facts require evidence; product choices require the human.
Pattyline publishes drafts for human reconciliation before changing project-wide
vocabulary. Completion means every settled new term and consequential trade-off
has a durable representation and unresolved disagreements remain visible.
