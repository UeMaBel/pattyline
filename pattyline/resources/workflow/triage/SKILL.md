---
name: triage
description: Refine adopted or proposed implementation tickets without granting execution approval.
---

Validate immutable feature/number identity, task type, explicit local/cloud route,
source specification, scope, acceptance criteria, dependencies, structured named
checks and escalation policy. Reject cycles, unknown references and contradictory
fields. Preserve source histories and mappings. Historical claimed/resolved
labels establish neither an active worker nor verified integration. Missing
requirements return to refinement. Reconstructed specifications remain labeled
drafts. Only a human approval of effective requirements makes a ticket ready.
